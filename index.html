Beomjin Seo

AI Engineer · Seoul, South Korea

I'm currently an LLM/VLM engineer at Samsung Research, where I develop on-device AI models for Samsung smartphones used by millions of people worldwide. My work focuses primarily on the pre/post-training of VLMs, with an emphasis on hardware-friendly & efficient architectures and post-training methods.

Please feel free to reach out, whether you'd like to talk about research or just have a casual chat!

Personally, I hold the following perspectives:

  1. LM understanding
    • LMs are essentially memory banks, and pre-training methods are different ways of injecting inductive biases into that memory
      e.g., Transformers, Mamba, MoE, recurrent-depth, and so on.
    • Retrieval strategy matters too: autoregressive and diffusion-based methods are different ways to retrieve information, and post-training methods are different ways to retrieve the knowledge already encoded in the model.
  2. LM expansion for application tasks
    • Exposure to diverse environments during training is key - not just non-physical data (e.g., the internet), but physical environments as well.
    • Unfamiliar distribution shifts make modality expansion challenging - models often struggle to decide whether to rely on the language prior.
