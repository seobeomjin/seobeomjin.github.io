AI Engineer · Seoul, South Korea

I'm currently an LLM/VLM engineer at Samsung Research, where I develop on-device AI models for Samsung smartphones used by millions of people worldwide. My work focuses primarily on the pre/post-training of VLMs, with an emphasis on hardware-friendly & efficient architectures and post-training methods.

Please feel free to reach out, whether you'd like to talk research or just have a casual chat!

Personllay I have perspectives such as below:

  1. LM understanding
    • LM is memory bank and pre-training methodologies are variants to take inductive biases to memories
      e.g. Transformers, Mamba, MoE, or recurrent-depth and so on.
    • Retrieval strategy is significant. Auto-regressive method or diffusion methods are variants to retrieve. post-training methodologies are variants to retrieve their knowledges.
  2. LM expansion for application tasks
    • Seeing various environments during training session is the key. Even not only on non-physical things but also physical environments.
    • Unfamiliar distribution shifting is challenging to expand modalities. Models hesitate whether they should depend on language prior or not.
